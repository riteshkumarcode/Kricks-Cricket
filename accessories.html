Accessories.

Gloves, pads, helmets, kit bags, grips and balls. Everything you carry out to the middle, in Kricks colours.

Getting the fit right

Gloves
Your fingertips should sit just short of the end of the finger rolls. Men's fits most adult hands; youth fits players up to about 15.
Pads
The centre of the knee roll should sit on the centre of your knee when you stand normally.
Helmet
Measure around your head just above the eyebrows. The grille should not touch your nose when you look forward.
Grips and toe guards
Fit a fresh grip when the old one turns smooth or splits, and a toe guard on any bat used on damp grounds.
