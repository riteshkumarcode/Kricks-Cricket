Common questions

Do you ship outside India?

Yes. We ship to the UK, Australia, New Zealand, the UAE, South Africa, the USA, Canada and more. Shipping and any import duties are shown at checkout before you pay.

How long does delivery take?

Stock bats leave us within 3 working days. Delivery takes 3–6 days in India and 7–12 days internationally. Custom bats take 10–14 days to make first.

Can I return a bat?

Unused bats in their original condition can be returned within 14 days. Engraved custom bats can't be returned unless they arrive damaged.

Do I need to knock in my bat?

Every English willow bat needs knocking in before it faces a hard ball. Add our knocking-in service on the product page and we'll do it before shipping.

My bat has cracked. Can you help?

Small surface cracks on the face are normal and can be repaired. Send us photos and we'll tell you whether it's a repair or a warranty case.
