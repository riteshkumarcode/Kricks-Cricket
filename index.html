Made by hand

Every Kricks bat goes from a split cleft of willow to a knocked-in bat in five steps, each done by hand.

  1. 01CleftWillow is split along the grain, never sawn across it.
  2. 02Air-driedClefts dry slowly until the moisture is right for pressing.
  3. 03PressedRollers compress the face so it holds together under the ball.
  4. 04ShapedSpine, edges and toe are shaped with draw-knife and spokeshave.
  5. 05Knocked inOiled and knocked in with a mallet, ready for the nets.
