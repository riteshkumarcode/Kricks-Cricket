Knocking in your bat

  1. Rub a teaspoon of raw linseed oil into the face, edges and toe. Leave it to soak in overnight. Never oil the splice or the sticker.
  2. Tap the face with a bat mallet for about two hours, slowly building up force. Round the edges and toe gently.
  3. Face throw-downs with an old ball for a week, then play in the nets before using it in a match.
