Tennis bats.

Light bats for the tennis ball and the taped ball. Thin handles, scooped backs and low middles for gully, street and box cricket.

Tennis ball or tape ball?

Tennis ball
A soft ball that bounces high. Choose a light bat with a mid middle so you can get on top of the bounce.
Tape ball
Taped tennis balls skid through low and fast. A low middle and a flat face meet them sooner.
Box cricket
Short boundaries and quick hands. Our lightest bats, around 850–950 g, are made for it.
Never with a leather ball
Tennis bats are not pressed for the hard ball and will crack. Use a leather bat for that.
